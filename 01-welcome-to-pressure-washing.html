1.1 — Why Flamingo Is Adding Pressure Washing

Flamingo Window Pro built its reputation on careful, detail-oriented exterior cleaning. Pressure washing is a natural extension of that reputation — the same core values of safety, property protection, and customer trust, applied to a new set of surfaces and equipment. Customers who already trust Flamingo for their windows are often the first to ask whether we also handle their driveway, siding, deck, or roof.

1.2 — What Makes a Great Technician

Walk through each trait below and be specific about what it looks like on a real job.

  • Safety-first mindset — equipment, chemicals, ladders, and traffic all carry real risk if rushed
  • Respect for the property — pressure washing can damage surfaces, plants, and finishes if done carelessly
  • Attention to pressure and dilution — more is not better, and mistakes are expensive
  • Clear communication with customers before, during, and after the job
  • Willingness to slow down and double-check rather than rush a result

1.3 — How This Program Is Organized

This program has 15 modules, moving from safety fundamentals through equipment, chemicals, surface-specific technique, and finally professionalism and certification. Each module ends with a knowledge check and an in-person sign-off with a trainer.

1.4 — Company Resources

Every truck carries a chemical dilution chart, an eyewash bottle, and a laminated pressure/tip reference card. A trainee should know where each of these lives on the truck before their first supervised job.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Treating orientation like paperwork instead of culture-setting
  • Assuming a new hire already knows what 'careful' looks like on a jobsite
  • Letting a trainee handle equipment before Module 2 is signed off
  • Rushing through the resource walkthrough

💬 Real-World Scenario

A new trainee asks to 'just try the wand for a minute' on their first day, before any safety training. What should the trainer do?

Politely decline and explain that no one touches the equipment until Module 2: Safety Fundamentals & PPE is complete and signed off — this protects both the trainee and the company.
