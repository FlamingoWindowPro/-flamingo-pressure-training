Module 15 — Quality Control, Troubleshooting & Certification

Confirm a trainee is ready to represent Flamingo Window Pro on pressure washing jobs without supervision.

15.115.215.3

15.1 — Final Quality-Control Walkthrough

  • Check all treated surfaces from multiple angles and lighting for missed spots or streaking
  • Confirm landscaping has been fully rinsed and shows no signs of chemical stress
  • Confirm no property damage occurred
  • Photograph the finished result for the job file

15.2 — Common Problems & Fixes

  • Streaking on siding — usually incomplete rinse coverage; re-rinse top to bottom
  • Uneven concrete results — surface cleaner moved too slowly or unevenly; redo with consistent overlap
  • Plant damage — insufficient pre-wet or post-rinse; document and notify the customer immediately
  • Customer reports damage after the fact — document with pre-job photos, notify your lead the same day

15.3 — Certification Requirements

A trainee is eligible to work pressure washing jobs unsupervised only after:

  • All 15 module checklists and quizzes are complete
  • At least one fully supervised job of each major type: siding, roof soft-wash, and concrete
  • A final ride-along evaluation with a lead technician
  • Sign-off from a manager confirming certification
MODULE 15 CERTIFICATION: This is the final gate before independent work. A manager sign-off is required in addition to the trainer sign-off below.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the final quality-control walkthrough to save time
  • Not documenting a customer-reported issue with pre-job photos
  • Assuming quiz completion alone means certification is done
  • Moving to independent jobs without a manager sign-off

💬 Real-World Scenario

A customer calls two days after a job claiming a plant died from chemical exposure. What should the technician do?

Document the situation with the pre-job photos on file and notify their lead the same day — never attempt to resolve pricing or liability on-site or on the phone alone.
