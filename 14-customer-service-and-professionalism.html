Module 14 — Customer Service & On-Site Professionalism

Make every technician the best possible representative of the Flamingo brand on-site.

14.114.214.3

14.1 — Arrival & Walkthrough

  • Arrive in uniform, on time, with a clean and organized truck
  • Greet the customer, confirm scope, and walk the property together first
  • Point out existing damage or problem areas before starting
  • Set expectations on timing, noise, and areas that need to stay clear

14.2 — During the Job

  • Keep noise and disruption to a minimum, especially early morning or evening
  • If something unexpected comes up, pause and communicate rather than guessing
  • Never enter a customer's home unless specifically required and approved

14.3 — Departure

  • Do a final walkthrough with the customer, pointing out results and any follow-up areas
  • Confirm all equipment and chemical containers are loaded before leaving
  • Leave the property tidier than you found it
  • Thank the customer and share how to reach the company with questions

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the pre-job walkthrough and letting the customer discover damage later
  • Leaving equipment or containers behind at the job site
  • Entering a customer's home without explicit approval
  • Rushing the final walkthrough

💬 Real-World Scenario

Midway through a job, a technician notices something unexpected — a locked gate blocking the backyard. What should they do?

Pause and communicate with the customer rather than forcing access or guessing at a workaround.
