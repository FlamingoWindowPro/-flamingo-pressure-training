Module 6 — Chemicals & Detergents

Teach safe, correct chemical use before a trainee mixes anything without direct supervision.

6.16.26.3

6.1 — Common Chemicals

  • Sodium hypochlorite (SH) — kills algae, mold, and mildew (soft washing)
  • Surfactant (soap) — helps solution cling to vertical surfaces
  • Degreaser — driveways, garage floors, commercial pads
  • Rust and efflorescence removers — specialty stubborn staining
  • Neutralizer / rinse aid — protects metal and plants after treatment

6.2 — Dilution & Mixing

Every chemical has a posted mixing chart in the truck's chemical bin. Ratios change based on surface and severity of staining.

  • Always add chemical to water, not water to chemical
  • Always double-check the chart for the specific surface
  • Label mixed containers if not used immediately
  • Never combine SH-based products with anything containing ammonia

6.3 — Protecting the Property

  • Pre-wet all plants, shrubs, and grass before and after chemical use
  • Cover or move sensitive items — furniture, planters, fixtures, AC units
  • Close all windows and doors before applying chemical to siding
  • Never let solution run directly into storm drains where prohibited
MODULE 6 CERTIFICATION: No technician mixes chemical unsupervised until this module is signed off.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Assuming a container someone else mixed is at the correct dilution
  • Adding water to chemical instead of chemical to water
  • Forgetting to pre-wet landscaping before applying detergent
  • Mixing SH near an ammonia-based product

💬 Real-World Scenario

A technician finds a mixed chemical container on the truck with no label. What should they do?

Treat it as unknown and do not use it — remix from the chart rather than assuming the dilution is correct.
