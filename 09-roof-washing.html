Module 9 — Roof Washing (Soft Wash Only)

Cover the highest-risk task in this manual with the care it requires.

9.19.29.3

9.1 — Why Roofs Are Different

Asphalt shingles are coated with granules that shed under direct high pressure, shortening the roof's life and typically voiding the manufacturer's warranty. The dark streaking homeowners want removed is almost always algae, killed effectively by soft-wash chemical without mechanical force.

9.2 — Safe Roof Access

  • Never walk a roof with a wet or soaped surface
  • Use fall protection (harness + anchor) for any roof walking
  • Never work a roof in wind, rain, or an approaching storm
  • Confirm roof material first — tile, metal, and slate require lead sign-off

9.3 — Soft-Wash Roof Process

  • Cover or divert downspouts if there are rain barrels or irrigation connections
  • Pre-wet landscaping and siding below the roofline
  • Apply the roof-specific (weaker) chemical mix from ridge down, low pressure
  • Allow full dwell time; most treatments are low-pressure rinsed or rain-rinsed
  • Thoroughly re-rinse landscaping and hardscape below the roofline
MODULE 9 CERTIFICATION: No technician performs solo roof work until specifically signed off by a lead technician on this module.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Walking a roof after it's already been soaped
  • Using the siding chemical mix instead of the weaker roof mix
  • Working a roof when a storm is approaching
  • Skipping fall protection because 'it's a quick job'

💬 Real-World Scenario

A technician is asked to quickly touch up a roof but their harness is in another truck. What should they do?

Wait until proper fall protection is available. No roof work happens without it, regardless of how quick the job seems.
