Module 13 — Property Protection & Environmental Responsibility

Build the pre-job habits that prevent property damage and keep the company compliant with local rules.

13.113.213.3

13.1 — Pre-Job Protection Routine

  • Photograph the property before starting — existing damage, cracks, landscaping condition
  • Identify and pre-wet all plants, shrubs, and grass in the work path
  • Move, cover, or relocate furniture, décor, vehicles, and pet items
  • Confirm all doors and windows are closed before any chemical application
  • Identify storm drains, wells, and water features near the work area

13.2 — Runoff & Wastewater Practices

  • Follow local ordinance regarding wastewater and chemical runoff into storm drains
  • Use containment mats, socks, or a vacuum recovery system for chemical-heavy jobs where required
  • Never rinse directly into a pond, stream, well, or natural water feature
  • Dispose of leftover mixed chemical per the product label

13.3 — Talking to Customers

Technicians should be able to explain, in plain terms, that Flamingo pre-wets and rinses landscaping to protect plants, uses the minimum effective dilution, and follows local runoff regulations — without overstating claims the company can't back up.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Skipping pre-job photos and having no record if damage is later disputed
  • Not identifying storm drains before starting a chemical-heavy job
  • Rinsing chemical runoff directly into a pond or stream
  • Overstating environmental claims to a customer

💬 Real-World Scenario

A customer asks if the chemicals used are 'totally safe for the environment.' How should a technician respond?

Explain honestly that Flamingo uses the minimum effective dilution, pre-wets and rinses landscaping to protect plants, and follows local runoff regulations — without overstating a claim the company can't back up.
