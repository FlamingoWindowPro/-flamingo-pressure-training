Module 12 — Gutter Brightening & Exterior Add-On Services

Perform common add-on services correctly and understand how they're approved and quoted.

12.112.212.3

12.1 — Gutter Brightening

Gutter brightening targets the black streaking on the exterior face of gutters — oxidation, not dirt — which doesn't come off with plain water.

  • Pre-wet siding and landscaping below the gutter line
  • Apply brightener directly to the streaked area at low pressure
  • Allow full dwell time per the product label before rinsing
  • Rinse thoroughly, checking nothing runs down and dries on siding below

12.2 — Other Common Add-Ons

  • Window screen rinse-off (coordinate with the window cleaning team)
  • Outdoor furniture and cushion washing (low pressure, mild detergent only)
  • Garage floor degreasing
  • Mailbox and post cleaning
  • Solar panel exterior rinse (low pressure only, only if approved for the panel model)

12.3 — Quoting & Communicating Add-Ons

Add-ons should always be discussed with the customer before starting, even if a technician notices an obvious opportunity on-site. Never begin unapproved chargeable work — offer it, get a yes, and only then proceed.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Starting an add-on before the customer approves it
  • Letting gutter brightener run down and dry on siding below
  • Using high pressure on outdoor furniture or cushions
  • Rinsing solar panels without confirming they're approved for it

💬 Real-World Scenario

A technician notices badly streaked gutters that weren't part of the quoted job. What should they do?

Point it out to the customer as an opportunity and get their approval before doing any work — never start unapproved chargeable work, even with good intentions.
